# Public boundary

This repo is a **curated, anonymized projection** of private delivery work. It is not a mirror of any private catalog, client repo, or internal Build Library.

## Never ship

- Client legal names, logos, screenshots, or internal codenames
- Employer brand names, org charts, exec names, or internal tool/pod codenames
- Private repository links (`rg-tx/*`, unlisted remotes, contract folders)
- Unverified metrics (pilot ROI, ERP write rates, verdict slice counts, Finance-internal $)
- OEM manuals, claim sheets, CMM extracts, or controlled corpus content
- PII, credentials, `.env` values, tenant schemas, or ERP contracts
- Contracts, stubs, toys, or internal scoring systems
- Branded executive prototypes (account-review demos with employer design systems)

## Qualitative outcomes only

Prefer outcomes like “human confirm before ERP write,” “undetermined is a first-class verdict,” “print fidelity as a product requirement,” or “first automated legal gate for scaled GenAI content.”

Do not invent numbers. Do not publish Jester slice counts, pilot success rates, or enterprise `$` / model-count figures without explicit confirmation. Prefer ranges or altitude language (“nine-figure portfolio,” “lean model ops”) until cleared.

## Allowed public proof

- This site (Cloudflare Pages / GitHub source)
- Public open-source kits (e.g. [claude-html-kit](https://github.com/rmgtx/claude-html-kit), [PageEX](https://github.com/rmgtx/PageEX))
- Abstract architecture diagrams (no client UI, no real documents)
- Sanitized case-study copy rewritten from resume clusters
- White-labeled enterprise themes (roles, years, patterns of work)

## Review checklist before any new public page

- [ ] Client / employer names anonymized per cheat-sheet

- [ ] No metrics without explicit confirmation
